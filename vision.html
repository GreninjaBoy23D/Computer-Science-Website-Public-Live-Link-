04 — MY VISION

My Vision:
Technology That Helps.

My goal is to use computer science skills to help people navigate technology, solve problems, and contribute to projects that make a meaningful difference.

01

Make technology more approachable.

Technology can be confusing or inaccessible. I want to help people understand the tools available to them and feel more confident using them.

◎
02

Contribute to useful projects.

I hope to collaborate on projects that address real needs, whether that means building a helpful tool, improving an existing experience, or supporting a team with technical skills.

⌘
03

Keep learning and sharing.

Good work comes from curiosity and collaboration. I want to keep developing my skills, share what I learn, and stay open to better ways of doing things.

✳
→

My ambition is simple: use what I learn to make technology a little more useful, understandable, and welcoming for others.
